Delete Your Sport Pikr Account

Last updated: July 2026
⚠️ This is permanent. Deleting your account removes your profile, favourite teams, tip history, achievements, trophies, and group memberships. Groups you created will also be deleted for every member. This cannot be undone.

Option 1: Delete in the app (recommended)

  1. Open the Sport Pikr app and log in to your account.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Scroll to the Danger Zone section and tap 🗑️ Delete account.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm.
  5. Your account and all associated data are removed immediately.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app installed or can't log in, email sportpikr@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account and request account deletion.

Please include your username so we can locate your account. We'll confirm once it's deleted, typically within a few business days.

What gets deleted

  • Profile information (username, display name, email, avatar, bio)
  • Favourite teams and app preferences
  • Tip/prediction history and leaderboard standings
  • Achievements and trophies
  • Group memberships — and any groups you created are deleted for all members
  • Messages you've sent within groups
  • Push notification device tokens

What may be retained

  • Subscription/payment transaction records may be retained by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) as required for tax, accounting, or fraud-prevention purposes, independent of your Sport Pikr account.
  • Aggregated, de-identified usage data that can no longer be linked back to you.
