Privacy Policy

Sport Pikr ("we", "us", or "our") is operated by Jack Post (ABN 63 356 352 539), trading as Sport Pikr, based in Brisbane, Australia.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use the Sport Pikr mobile application.

Last updated: 1 July 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Name, email address, and password when you register an account
  • Profile photo if you choose to upload one
  • Your tipping selections and group activity within the app
  • Payment information processed securely via Stripe (we do not store card details)
  • Device information and push notification tokens for sending alerts

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Operate tipping competitions and group features
  • Send you push notifications about your tips and group activity
  • Process payments for premium subscriptions
  • Improve the app and troubleshoot issues
  • Communicate with you about your account or updates to the app

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Supabase – our backend database and authentication provider
  • Stripe – for secure payment processing
  • Firebase (Google) – for push notification delivery
  • Expo – for app distribution and updates

All third-party providers are bound by their own privacy policies and applicable data protection laws.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase infrastructure. We use industry-standard security measures including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and secure authentication. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Opt out of push notifications via your device settings

To exercise these rights, contact us at sportpikr@gmail.com.

6. Children's Privacy

Sport Pikr is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
